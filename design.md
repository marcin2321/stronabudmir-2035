# Budmir — design direction

Approved: responsive frontend website in packages/web with full PL (default) and DE copy. English was removed at the user's request. Preserve the logo, company contact details and factual services. No database or accounts.

## Visual system
- Warm white #f5f4ef, graphite #20221f, muted gray #787b73, construction yellow #f4d349. Preserve blue in original logo.
- Barlow Condensed headlines, Manrope body. Square geometry, fine dividers, uppercase section labels.
- Generous 6% gutters and 100px section spacing. Architectural photography, asymmetric dark hero overlay and oversized typography.
- Image-led service columns, dark about section, filterable gallery, yellow contact invitation. No rounded card grid.

## Interactions
- Motion staggered copy, slow image scale, scroll parallax, viewport reveals and hover zoom. Honor reduced-motion.
- Persist language and update document metadata. Mobile navigation, gallery category filters and accessible lightbox.
- Contact form opens a prefilled email draft; explicitly label this, never claim delivery.
- Original site photography includes stock imagery: label gallery as examples/inspiration, not verified projects.

## Reference
The Facebook reference was subsequently retrieved and inspected: a pinned scroll-controlled architectural fly-through with camera zoom and sequential interior chapters.

## Scroll presentation update
Preserve the approved page below the hero and PL/DE controls. Replace the shallow hero parallax with a four-chapter, full-viewport pinned presentation. Native scrolling scrubs photo zoom, perspective-like panning, layered transitions and chapter typography; scrolling backward reverses it. Provide chapter buttons and a skip-to-services link, support mobile touch, and bypass pinning for reduced-motion users. Use only existing Budmir photos: this reproduces the scroll/slide mechanic, not continuous 3D footage, since no clean walkthrough source was supplied.
